# Prompts

Formula: WHAT + INTERACTION + STYLE (medium, palette, texture, render).


## Portfolio

Build a Three.js portfolio for my [work]. Each project is a floating object in a dark room. Click flies the camera to it and opens details.

## Game

Make a Three.js endless runner. Low poly road, WASD to dodge, score in the corner, speed rises every 10 s. One file.

## Music

Audio-reactive scene: load an mp3, use AnalyserNode, bass scales a sphere, highs spawn particles. Bloom on.

## Webcam

Use the webcam as a texture on a plane. Add datamosh and RGB split that spike when motion is detected.

## Product

Show my GLB product on a turntable. Studio lighting, orbit controls, color swatches that swap the material.

## Data

Plot this CSV as 3D bars on a grid. Hover shows the value. Camera eases to the tallest bar on load.

## Multiplayer

Shared room with Three.js + WebSockets. Each visitor is a glowing cube that moves with the arrow keys.

## Shader

Full-screen fragment shader of flowing ink. Mouse pushes the fluid. Black and white, film grain.


## Pop-up book portfolio (Fable 5.2)

Three.js portfolio as a pop-up book. One app per spread, cut-paper layers stand up as the page turns. Two-ink risograph print, halftone, soft shadows. Drag to turn, tap an icon to open it.

## Chladni plate (Fable 5.2)

Chladni plate in Three.js. 46,000 sand grains jitter by local plate amplitude and settle on the nodal lines. Drag to tune the mode, play the tone. Low-res render, 1-bit Bayer dither.
